# India design-partner shortlist

_Researched 14 October 2026 from organizations' own public pages. This is a hypothesis-led research list, not a list of customers, confirmed buyers, contacts, intent or permission to approach anyone._

## Selection rule

Prioritize India-based teams that publicly describe software delivery or platform engineering at multi-repository scale. The relevant initial conversation is with a **platform engineering, developer experience, release engineering or DevSecOps owner**, followed by the release approver. Each entry needs qualification against its present workflow; published descriptions do not establish demand for `deliver`.

## Qualification workflow

1. Verify current team ownership and public channels before any outreach. Do not infer a person's current role from an old job posting.
2. Ask each team which release decision is slow or disputed, what system runs checks today, who owns the scripts, and what evidence an approver needs.
3. Offer a local two-repository pilot only if there is a named owner, a measurable review burden and approval to run third-party CLI software.
4. Log outcomes as **uncontacted → interviewed → qualified → pilot agreed → active → converted/declined**. Keep notes and any contact data in an approved system, not this repository.

**Current state:** all seven organizations are **uncontacted**. No buyer interview or pilot has occurred, and this list alone does not satisfy the roadmap's demand-validation gate.
